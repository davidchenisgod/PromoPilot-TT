# PromoPilot TT — First customer outreach

Use these as starting points. Personalize each message and contact businesses through publicly listed business channels. Do not mass-spam or claim you have worked with brands you have not served.

## First message

Hi [business name]! I came across your page and wanted to introduce PromoPilot TT. We create affordable promotional graphics and ready-to-post captions for businesses and brands in Trinidad & Tobago.

We're opening a few introductory slots and I thought your [specific product/service/post] could work well with a fresh promo design.

Would you be interested in seeing a sample concept or our package details? No pressure at all.

## If they ask for prices

Thanks for asking! Our proposed introductory packages are:

- Starter: 3 graphics + 3 captions — TT$100
- Growth: 8 graphics + 8 captions — TT$250
- Monthly: 12 graphics + 12 captions + a simple content plan — TT$400

We'll confirm exactly what you need, the turnaround and final price before any payment or work begins.

## Follow-up (send once, after a few days)

Hi [name], just following up on my message about promotional graphics for [business]. If it's not something you're looking for right now, no worries. Happy to share the package details if useful.

## Questions to qualify an enquiry

1. What product, service or offer do you want to promote?
2. Which platform(s) will you post on?
3. Do you have a logo, brand colours and photos we should use?
4. When do you need the content?
5. Which package or approximate budget suits you?

## Important operating notes

- Never create a free custom design that could be mistaken for approved client work. A small watermarked sample concept is enough if you choose to offer one.
- Do not start paid work until scope, deadline, payment and revision limits are agreed.
- Ask permission before using customer work in a public portfolio.
- Never promise followers, reach, engagement or sales.
