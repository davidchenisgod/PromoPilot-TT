# First-week launch plan

## Objective

Validate whether businesses and brands will pay for clear, affordable promotional graphics and captions. The first milestone is one paid pilot order, not follower count or website traffic.

## Day 1 — Make the offer real
- Confirm contact channel and payment method.
- Review the proposed package scope and pricing.
- Test website on a phone.
- Prepare three sample concepts for different business categories.

## Day 2 — Build a prospect list
- Identify 15–20 small businesses with active public social pages and visible promotional needs.
- Record business name, public contact channel, why the offer is relevant, and date contacted.
- Prioritize businesses with a current menu, service list, event, seasonal offer or product launch.

## Day 3 — Personalized outreach
- Send 5–8 individually personalized introductions.
- Ask permission before sending a sample.
- Track replies and questions.

## Day 4 — Follow up and learn
- Send one polite follow-up where appropriate.
- Note objections: price, trust, need, timing, existing designer.
- Do not pressure people who decline.

## Day 5 — Offer a paid pilot
- Offer one clearly scoped Starter package.
- Confirm deliverables, deadline, revision limit and payment terms in writing.
- Do not promise results beyond the deliverables.

## Day 6 — Deliver carefully
- Use customer-provided or properly licensed assets.
- Confirm spelling, prices, dates, contact details and platform dimensions.
- Get customer approval before final delivery.

## Day 7 — Review
Track:
- Businesses contacted
- Replies
- Interested leads
- Quotes sent
- Paid orders
- Time spent per order
- Common objections and requested changes

Change pricing or positioning only based on actual feedback.

## Constraints

This plan assumes no paid advertising, no subscriptions and no purchased domain. Payment availability and fees must be confirmed with the provider before accepting orders.
