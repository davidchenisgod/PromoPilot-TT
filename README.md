# PromoPilot TT

A social media promotion service for businesses and brands in Trinidad and Tobago.

**Positioning:** Affordable, clear, ready-to-post promotional content for businesses, brands and entrepreneurs.

## Current offer (proposed introductory pricing)

| Package | Deliverables | Proposed price |
|---|---|---:|
| Starter | 3 graphics + 3 captions + 1 revision round | TT$100 |
| Growth | 8 graphics + 8 captions + 2 revision rounds | TT$250 |
| Monthly | 12 graphics + 12 captions + simple content plan + 2 revision rounds | TT$400 |

These are test prices, not validated market rates. Confirm scope, turnaround, revisions and payment terms in writing before accepting an order. Do not promise engagement, reach, sales or specific business results.

## Website

- `index.html` — responsive landing page
- `styles.css` — visual system and responsive styling
- `script.js` — mobile navigation and enquiry message preparation

The enquiry form currently prepares and copies a message locally. It does **not** transmit, store or email customer information. A real contact channel and order/payment workflow must be configured before public launch.

## Deploy with GitHub Pages

1. Open repository Settings.
2. Select Pages.
3. Under Build and deployment, choose Deploy from a branch.
4. Select `main` and `/(root)`, then Save.
5. Wait for GitHub Pages to publish the site.

## Operating principles

- Start with one paid pilot customer and learn from the work.
- Obtain permission before using a customer's logo, photos or brand assets.
- Use licensed or customer-provided images; do not imply sample concepts are client work.
- Confirm deliverables, deadline, revisions and payment before starting.
- Keep customer contact details and order files out of this public repository.
- No paid subscriptions, advertising or domain purchases until validated demand supports them.

## Launch checklist

- [ ] Choose and configure a business contact channel.
- [ ] Confirm local payment method and written payment terms.
- [ ] Test the website on mobile and desktop.
- [ ] Publish through GitHub Pages.
- [ ] Prepare 3 sample graphics as downloadable portfolio images.
- [ ] Contact a relevant list of businesses with personalized messages.
- [ ] Record replies, objections and paid conversions to validate pricing.
